Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how TIDS – Google Ads Automation accesses, uses and handles information when a user connects their Google account and uses the application.

About the application

TIDS – Google Ads Automation is a student project developed for educational and development purposes. The application assists users with researching keywords, generating advertising content and creating Google Ads search campaigns.

Google user data

The application may access Google Ads data only after the user authorizes access through Google's OAuth authorization process.

Google Ads data may be used to perform functions requested by the user, including keyword research and the creation and management of Google Ads campaign components.

How data is used

Data obtained through Google APIs is used only to provide the functionality of the application requested by the user.

The application does not sell Google user data or use Google user data for advertising, profiling or unrelated purposes.

Data sharing

Google user data is not sold to third parties. Data is only processed as necessary to provide the application's functionality and communicate with the services used by the application.

Data storage

The application does not intentionally store Google user data longer than necessary to perform the requested functionality.

User control and revocation

Users can revoke the application's access to their Google account at any time through their Google Account security settings.

Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information processed by the application and to prevent unauthorized access or disclosure.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the functionality of the application or its use of Google APIs changes.
