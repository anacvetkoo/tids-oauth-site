TIDS – Google Ads Automation

TIDS – Google Ads Automation is a student project that automates the preparation and creation of Google Ads search campaigns.

About the application

The application analyzes the content of a user-provided landing page, researches relevant keywords, analyzes search results and uses AI to generate suitable advertising copy.

The generated campaign settings, keywords and advertising content can then be used to create a Google Ads search campaign through the Google Ads API.

Purpose

This application is being developed as part of a university student project for educational and development purposes.

Google Ads integration

Google Ads access is used to research keyword ideas and to create and manage campaign components requested by the user.

The application does not create or modify Google Ads campaigns without user authorization.
